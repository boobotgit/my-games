Boo's Chicken Chase

Boo is hungry, and there are chickens loose in the yard.

  • 📱Tilt the phone to steer Boo. The further you tilt, the faster Boo runs. Hold it level and Boo sits.
  • 🧭The yard is a diamond, so tilt toward one of its four corners to pick a direction.
  • 🐔Catch every chicken to clear the level.
  • 💩Every chicken comes back out the other end. Don't step in it!

iPhone will ask to use Motion & Orientation.
