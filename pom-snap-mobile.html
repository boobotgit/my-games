How to play

  • Find a Pom: point the camera down at the floor, grass, bed or couch and hold still for a moment.
  • Tap the ground to toss a treat there. Tap the Pom to pet it.
  • Call whistles the Pom over to sit in front of you, which is great for close-ups.
  • Snap any time. Rarer poses score more, and so does a Pom that's looking at you and fills the frame.
  • Surface wrong? Tap the surface chip at the top to pick it yourself.
  • Pom looks too big, tiny or floaty? Pinch the screen to set how far away the surface is.
  • Hold the phone steady and turn it, but don't walk around. The Pom stays put when you turn and follows you if you walk.
  • ✕ on the Pom's name tag says goodbye so you can find a different one.
